Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a personal budgeting application built and used by a single individual (the developer) for their own private use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the application reads the owner's own bank account balances and transactions through the Enable Banking API (Open Banking). Access is read-only.

How it is used

The data is used only to display the owner's own balances and spending in the application. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Data is stored only on the owner's own device(s) and is not uploaded to any external service, other than the request to Enable Banking needed to fetch it.

Your rights

Because the only person whose data is processed is the owner, the owner can delete the data or revoke bank consent at any time through their bank or the Enable Banking Control Panel.
